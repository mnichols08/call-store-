# If the call desk stops working

*One page for the owner and the counter staff. Fill in the two blanks and print it.*

## First: the shop keeps working

The phone still rings and the counter still sells. The call desk only adds to how the shop works now, so if it is down, take calls on paper and the register as before. Nothing the shop depends on is stored only in the call desk.

## What to do

1. Try again in a minute. A restart takes a few seconds.
2. Still down? Text **[NAME] at [PHONE]** with what you see on the screen.
3. Keep taking calls on paper. Write the customer, the part and a callback number. Enter them later if you like, or skip it.

## What you can count on

- **Replies:** during store hours, an answer within **[30] minutes**; outside hours, by the next morning.
- **Fix time:** most problems are a restart or a roll back to yesterday's version, done in minutes.
- **Your data:** a copy of everything is saved every night and kept for two weeks. The worst case is losing a few hours of calls, never the shop's customer list.
- **Watching:** the site is checked automatically, and a text goes to **[NAME]** if it stops answering, usually before anyone at the counter notices.

## You are never locked in

The shop owns the code and the hosting account. If [NAME] were ever unavailable, another developer can take over from the written setup guide in about an afternoon. Stopping is easy too: the customer list can be exported, and the shop is back on paper the same day.
